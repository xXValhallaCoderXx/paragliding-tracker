# Isolated Android social acceptance — 21 September 2026

The completed checks below passed on Samsung SM-S938B using three disposable pilots and an
isolated **Flight Log QA** installation. They exercise the native Friends, shared-flight replay
and kudos experience against the hosted backend. Native manual publication, offline Hide across
restart, explicit re-share and final cleanup also passed. No application-code fix was needed.

Feature contracts and earlier automated/API results remain in [Friends](./friends.md),
[shared flights](./shared-flights.md) and [kudos](./kudos.md). This report records the additional
phone checks, without counting earlier HTTP assertions as new native acceptance.

## Remaining limits

- The supporter list reached two entries. Native 25-row pagination, later pages and cursor
  changes were not exercised; the existing automated/API pagination evidence remains separate.
- Asha's first publication, initial Cora reaction and several counterparty operations used
  APIs. The second flight supplies the separately recorded native author flow. Ben did not
  sign back in natively to view that second publication; its friend-side readback was through APIs.
- Replay used a short synthetic IGC artifact. Native capture, automatic publication of a new
  recording, recorded-fix provenance, long/gapped routes, transfer interruption, recorder
  process-death recovery and recorder-priority behavior were not exercised in this session.
  The process-restart evidence here covers the durable Hide request only.
- Authorization loss was checked on subsequent access/refresh. This does not establish push
  revocation of an already-open view; the product has no realtime revocation channel.
- The isolated build caveat above remains even after these cases pass. No exact-owner-APK full
  acceptance, multi-phone concurrency, iOS or in-flight reliability claim is made.
